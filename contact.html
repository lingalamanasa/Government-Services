Support FAQ

Common Support Questions

What is the response time for email enquiries?
+

Email enquiries are typically answered within 24 hours on business days (Monday to Saturday). For urgent matters, please call our toll-free number 1800-111-555.

Can I escalate my grievance if not resolved?
+

Yes. If your grievance is not resolved within 7 working days, you can escalate it to the Nodal Grievance Officer or file a complaint on the CPGRAMS (Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System) portal.

How do I report a fraudulent service or impersonation?
+

Report immediately to fraud@govservices.in or call 1800-111-999 (Fraud Helpline). Do NOT share OTPs, passwords or Aadhaar details with anyone claiming to be our agent.

Is there a mobile app for Stackly GovServices?
+

Yes! Our app is available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store. Search "GovServices Stackly" and download for free. Available in 22 Indian languages.
