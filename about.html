Our Journey

A Decade of Digital Transformation

From a pilot program to India's largest digital government platform

2016
🚀 Foundation

Stackly GovServices was launched as a pilot in 3 states with 10 basic services. The vision: one portal for all government services.

2018
📱 Mobile First

Launched our mobile app with vernacular language support in 12 languages. Crossed 1 million registered users. Integrated with Aadhaar.

2020
🤝 National Expansion

Expanded to all 28 states. Launched during COVID-19 to deliver relief services digitally to millions of stranded citizens across India.

2022
🤖 AI Integration

Integrated AI-powered document verification, chatbot support in 22 languages, and predictive service recommendations for citizens.

2024
🏆 50M Citizens Milestone

Crossed 50 Million active citizens. Won the UN Public Service Award for digital innovation. Launched 500th service on the platform.

2026 — TODAY
🌟 The Future Is Now

Real-time service tracking, blockchain-verified certificates, and voice-enabled service access in 22 Indian languages. The journey continues.
